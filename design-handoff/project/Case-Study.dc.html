01 — Product Design

Fintech Dashboard Redesign

A cleaner, data-dense interface that helps finance teams track spend across departments without digging through spreadsheets.

Role
Product & UI Design
Year
2026
Tools
Figma, React, Framer

The problem

The existing dashboard buried spend data three clicks deep in dense tables. Finance teams were exporting to spreadsheets just to answer simple questions like "what did we spend on tools this quarter?"

Process

01
Research
Sat with the finance team to watch how they actually used the old dashboard.
02
Wireframes
Sketched a handful of layouts that surface totals and trends before the raw table.
03
Visual design
Built the component system in Figma, then in React so it could ship without a rebuild.
04
Handoff
Documented the component library so the team could extend it after launch.
